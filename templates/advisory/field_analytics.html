    📚 Understanding Your Analytics
    Key metrics and their calculations explained
    💧 Water Use Efficiency

    What it means: How effectively water is being used for crop growth.

    How it's calculated: (Effective water use / Total water applied) × 100

    Scale: 80-100% Excellent | 60-79% Good | Below 60% Needs improvement

    🔄 Irrigation Frequency

    What it means: Percentage of advisories that recommended irrigation.

    How it's calculated: (Number of irrigation recommendations / Total advisories) × 100

    Interpretation: Higher frequency may indicate water stress or low rainfall periods.

    📊 Data Quality Score

    What it means: Reliability of the data used for analysis.

    How it's calculated: Based on satellite data availability, weather station coverage, and model accuracy.

    Scale: 80-100% High quality | 60-79% Moderate | Below 60% Low quality

    🎯 Prediction Confidence

    What it means: How certain the system is about future irrigation needs.

    How it's calculated: Based on historical patterns, weather forecasts, and model validation.

    Factors: Crop stage, seasonal patterns, data quality, and weather forecast reliability.

    💰 Cost Savings

    What it means: Estimated monetary savings from optimized irrigation.

    How it's calculated: Water saved × Cost per unit + Energy savings + Labor optimization.

    Includes: Water costs, energy for pumping, labor time, and potential yield improvements.

    📈 Trends Analysis

    What it means: Direction of change in key parameters over time.

    How it's calculated: Statistical analysis of historical data using regression and moving averages.

    Parameters: ET₀ (evapotranspiration), rainfall patterns, and irrigation requirements.

    🛰️ Data Integration Process

    Our analytics combine multiple data sources for comprehensive field analysis:

    • Satellite Data: Google Earth Engine for vegetation and soil moisture indices
    • Weather Data: Real-time and forecast meteorological data
    • Crop Models: FAO-56 evapotranspiration calculations
    • Historical Analysis: Pattern recognition and trend analysis
    • Machine Learning: Predictive models for irrigation optimization
    • Quality Control: Data validation and uncertainty quantification
